Charging takes four steps.

  1. Drive in

    Pick a station in the app and park in a marked eDryv bay.

  2. Plug in

    Connect the cable. CCS2 and Type 2 at every site.

  3. Tap to pay

    Start the session in the app or with any contactless card.

  4. Charge up

    Watch the percentage from your phone. We'll ping you at 80%.
